AI2TH/Bible/Miktam Bible

Miktam Bible

Offline Bible study companion

Read and study Scripture anywhere, with no connection needed. Thousands of translations, an AI study assistant that runs on your device, Strong’s concordance and reading plans, all offline and private.

Get early access → Become a tester GitHub

Features

📖
5,600+ translations
Browse by book, chapter and verse; book names shown in the translation’s own language
🤖
AI study assistant
Ask about context and meaning with a local LLM. Runs fully offline; conversations stay on the device
🔍
Strong’s concordance
Original Greek and Hebrew word, transliteration, definition and every verse it appears in
🔎
Full-text search
Search the whole Bible instantly, grouped by book, with one-tap jump to the chapter
📅
Reading plans
Navigators 4-track, 90-day, 6-month and New Testament plans with reminders that follow your progress
🌅
Verse of the Day
Daily notification at midnight that matches the verse on the Home screen
⭐
Highlights and notes
Per-verse highlights, bookmarks and study notes that follow you across translations
🎙️
Voice memos
Record study notes tagged with the current book, chapter and verse

How it works

Everything happens on the phone. No account, no server, no connection needed after download.

React Native UI
Read, search, plans, notes, AI chat
↓
Bible and study databases
SQLite with FTS5: translations, Strong’s concordance, your notes
↓
On-device AI
Local GGUF language model; nothing leaves the phone
↓
Offline after download
KJV is bundled; other translations download once from the open Bible data repos
