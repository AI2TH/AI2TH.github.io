AI2TH

Applied Intelligence To Tackle Hardships.

What we do

AI2TH builds software that solves hard problems across different fields: a Linux workstation on Android, a Kubernetes IDE in your pocket, an offline Bible study companion, and encrypted software delivery.

Every project we ship is opinionated, focused, and built to work in practice — not just on paper. We don't add abstraction for its own sake. We build the minimum that gets the job done right.

Products

Each product keeps iterating, and related work merges into one home. Every Linux-on-Android project now lives in Linxr: Pockr, Zyvr and Kubxr were merged into it.
