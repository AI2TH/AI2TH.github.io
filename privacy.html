AI2TH/Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Transparency and security for the AI2TH ecosystem.

Privacy by design

AI2TH builds local-first software. Our Android apps, Linxr, Vizxr and Miktam Bible, run on your device, and your data stays there.

Data collection

We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

None of the apps include analytics, tracking or crash-reporting libraries that send data to external servers. Logs, notes, recordings and settings stay in the app’s storage on your phone.

Linxr permissions

Linxr runs Alpine Linux inside a QEMU virtual machine, isolated from your Android system. It asks for:

  • Internet: network access for the VM, for example SSH, apk add or pulling container images.
  • Foreground service: keeps the VM running while the app is in the background.
  • Notifications: shows the ongoing notification while a VM is running.
  • All files access: only to share a folder you pick with the VM over Virtio-9P. Files stay on your device.

Vizxr permissions

  • Internet and local network: to reach the Kubernetes clusters you add, including Linxr on localhost.
  • Storage: to import the kubeconfig files you choose.

Cluster credentials stay on your phone and are sent only to the clusters they belong to.

Miktam Bible permissions

  • Microphone: only when you record a voice memo. Recordings stay on your phone.
  • Notifications and run at startup: for reading-plan reminders and the Verse of the Day, rescheduled after a restart.
  • Internet: to download translations you choose. Reading, search and the AI assistant then work offline.
