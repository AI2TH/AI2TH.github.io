Linxr

Linux on Android

A full Alpine Linux workstation in one APK, no root. The home for every Linux-on-Android project: Pockr, Zyvr and Kubxr are merged in, and new ones land here too.

  • Containers (FastTrack OCI or Docker)
  • k3s Kubernetes and Asterisk PBX
  • Linux desktop GUI and SSH terminal
  • 1-click Marketplace with community recipes
v3.4.0 · Google Play closed testing · APK on GitHub
Details → GitHub

Vizxr

Kubernetes · DevOps

A Kubernetes IDE in your pocket. Inspect, debug and operate clusters from your phone, whether that's Linxr's local k3s or AKS, GKE and EKS.

  • Lens-style resource inspector
  • Live CPU and memory sparklines
  • CRD explorer and Helm releases
  • Logs, pod exec and YAML editor
v1.1.0 · APK on GitHub
Details → GitHub

Miktam Bible

Faith · Study

An offline Bible study companion with thousands of translations and an AI study assistant that runs entirely on your device.

  • 5,600+ translations, fully offline
  • On-device AI study assistant
  • Strong's concordance and full-text search
  • Reading plans, reminders and streaks
v1.2.1 · Google Play closed testing
Details → Early access

cryptship

Software delivery

Ship encrypted, license-gated application code inside Docker images. The image is ciphertext; a small loader runs your app only with a valid license.

  • AES-256-GCM payloads, Ed25519 licenses
  • Air-gapped, on-prem and cloud modes
  • Instant revocation via license server
  • Hardening and per-license watermarks
v0.1.0 · Go · GPLv3
Details → GitHub

Get our Play Store apps first

AI2TH’s Google Play apps, Linxr and Miktam Bible today, are released through closed testing. Join the AI2TH Google Group and you become a closed tester for all of them, including every new Play app as it ships.

  1. Join the AI2TH Google Group at groups.google.com/g/ai2th with the Google account on your phone.
  2. Become a tester once per app: Linxr · Miktam Bible.
  3. Install from Google Play. Updates arrive like any other app.

What’s new

Recently built and released across AI2TH.

  1. cryptship v0.1.012 Nov 2026
    cryptship first release

    Encrypted, license-gated app code in Docker images: offline, online and attested key providers, a reference license server with instant revocation, and three runnable demos.

  2. Linxr v3.4.06 Nov 2026
    Mobile Linux virtual desktop

    Run graphical X11, GTK and Qt apps on your phone. Openbox or XFCE4, viewed in-app through noVNC, with a resolution picker and quick launchers.

  3. Vizxr v1.1.05 Nov 2026
    Kubernetes IDE overhaul

    Lens-style resource inspector, live CPU and memory sparklines, CRD explorer, and Helm releases with 1-tap rollback.

  4. Miktam Bible v1.2.15 Nov 2026
    Smarter reading plans

    Reminders that follow your progress, a midnight Verse of the Day, custom reminder times, and authentic curricula such as Navigators 4-track and 90-day plans.

  5. Linxr v3.3.02 Nov 2026
    Zyvr merged into Linxr

    Asterisk 20 PBX now installs from the Linxr Marketplace, as a container or a native Alpine service. Also new: Zabbix, and native Nginx, PostgreSQL 16 and Redis.

  6. Linxr v3.2.01 Nov 2026
    FastTrack OCI engine

    Daemonless podman and crun containers cut idle CPU to under 1%; multi-core Cortex-A76 VM; full container lifecycle; k3s TLS handshakes from ~11 s to ~15 ms.

  7. Linxr v3.1.024 Oct 2026
    1-click Marketplace and Ollama AI

    Deploy k3s, Ollama, Nginx, Redis, PostgreSQL, VS Code Server and more in one tap, with recipes synced from linxr-marketplace.
