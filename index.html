What’s new

Recently built and released. Pockr, Zyvr and Kubxr are now merged into Linxr.

  1. Linxr v3.4.06 Nov 2026
    Mobile Linux virtual desktop

    Run graphical X11, GTK and Qt apps on your phone. Openbox or XFCE4 desktop, viewed in-app through noVNC, with a resolution picker and quick launchers.

  2. Vizxr v1.1.05 Nov 2026
    Kubernetes IDE for Android

    Lens-style resource inspector, live CPU and memory sparklines, CRD explorer, and Helm releases with 1-tap rollback. Works with Linxr’s k3s and remote clusters.

  3. Linxr v3.3.02 Nov 2026
    Asterisk PBX merged in, dual-mode Marketplace

    Zyvr’s Asterisk 20 PBX now installs from the Linxr Marketplace, as a container or as a native Alpine service. Also new: Zabbix, and native Nginx, PostgreSQL 16 and Redis.

  4. Linxr v3.2.01 Nov 2026
    FastTrack OCI engine and multi-core VM

    Daemonless containers with podman and crun cut idle CPU to under 1%. QEMU now runs multi-threaded on Cortex-A76. Adds full container lifecycle, custom port forwarding and faster k3s TLS.

  5. Linxr v3.1.024 Oct 2026
    1-click Marketplace and Ollama AI

    Deploy k3s Kubernetes, Ollama, Nginx, Redis, PostgreSQL, VS Code Server and more in one tap. Recipes sync from the community linxr-marketplace.
